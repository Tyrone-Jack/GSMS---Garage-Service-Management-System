Frequently Asked Questions

📅 Booking & Scheduling

How do I book a service?▼

Booking a service is easy! Follow these steps:

  1. Log in to your customer account
  2. Click on "Book Service" in the navigation menu
  3. Select the service you need (choose "Comprehensive Vehicle Inspection" if unsure)
  4. Select your vehicle or add a new one
  5. Choose your preferred date and time
  6. Review your booking details and confirm

You'll receive a confirmation email with your booking details.

Can I reschedule or cancel my booking?▼

Yes, you can modify or cancel your booking up to 24 hours before the scheduled time. To do this:

  1. Go to your Dashboard
  2. Find the booking under "Pending Bookings"
  3. Click "Modify" to reschedule or cancel

For cancellations less than 24 hours before the appointment, please contact us directly.

What are your operating hours?▼

Our service center operates:

  • Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Saturday: 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM
  • Sunday: Closed

We accept bookings during these hours. Same-day service may be available depending on availability.

🔧 Services & Pricing

What is included in the Comprehensive Vehicle Inspection?▼

The Comprehensive Vehicle Inspection includes a thorough check of:

  • Engine performance and diagnostics
  • Transmission system
  • Brake system (pads, rotors, fluid)
  • Suspension and steering components
  • Electrical system (battery, alternator, starter)
  • Cooling system
  • Fluid levels and conditions
  • Tire condition and pressure
  • Exhaust system

After the inspection, you'll receive a detailed report with recommended repairs and a quote.

Are your prices fixed or quoted?▼

Most of our standard services have fixed prices (e.g., Oil Change, Tire Rotation). However:

  • Comprehensive Vehicle Inspection: Price is quoted after inspection based on findings
  • Complex repairs: May require a quote after initial diagnosis
  • Parts replacement: Prices may vary based on your vehicle's make and model

You'll always receive a quote and approve it before any work begins.

Do you use genuine parts?▼

Yes, we use high-quality parts for all repairs. We offer:

  • Genuine OEM parts (Original Equipment Manufacturer)
  • High-quality aftermarket parts with warranty

You can choose your preference when we provide the quote, and we'll explain the differences in price and quality.

💰 Payments & Invoicing

What payment methods do you accept?▼

We accept multiple payment methods:

  • 💳 Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard)
  • 📱 M-Pesa (Paybill number provided on invoice)
  • 💵 Cash on pickup
  • 🏦 Bank Transfer

Payment is required upon completion of service unless prior arrangements have been made.

When will I receive my invoice?▼

Invoices are generated and available in your dashboard:

  • Immediately after service completion
  • Accessible under the "Invoices" section
  • Also sent to your registered email address

You can view, download, and print your invoices anytime from your account.

Do you offer warranties on your work?▼

Yes, we stand behind our work:

  • Labor warranty: 6 months or 10,000 km (whichever comes first)
  • Parts warranty: Varies by part (typically 12 months)
  • Battery warranty: 24 months

Warranty details are included on your invoice. Please keep your invoice for any warranty claims.

🚗 Vehicle Management

How many vehicles can I add to my account?▼

You can add an unlimited number of vehicles to your account. This is perfect for:

  • Families with multiple cars
  • Businesses with fleet vehicles
  • Car enthusiasts with multiple vehicles

Simply go to your Profile and click "Add Vehicle" to register a new vehicle.

Can I edit or remove a vehicle?▼

Yes, you can manage your vehicles anytime from your Profile page:

  • Edit: Update vehicle details (make, model, year, plate number)
  • Delete: Remove a vehicle (only possible if it has no active or past services)

If a vehicle has service history, it cannot be deleted but can be marked as inactive.

👤 Account Management

How do I change my password?▼

To change your password:

  1. Go to your Profile page
  2. Click "Change" under the Password section
  3. Enter your current password
  4. Enter and confirm your new password
  5. Click "Update Password"

Make sure your new password is at least 6 characters long and secure.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?▼

If you forgot your password:

  1. Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page
  2. Enter your registered email address
  3. Check your email for a password reset link
  4. Follow the link to create a new password

If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder or contact our support team.

How do I update my personal information?▼

To update your profile information:

  1. Go to your Profile page
  2. Click "Edit" under Personal Information
  3. Update your name, email, phone number, or location
  4. Click "Save Changes"

Your updated information will be reflected immediately.

How do I delete my account?▼

To delete your account:

  1. Go to your Profile page
  2. Scroll to "Account Settings"
  3. Click "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm your decision by typing "DELETE"

Warning: This action is permanent and will delete all your data including service history, vehicles, and invoices. Please make sure to download any important documents before proceeding.
